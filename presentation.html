01 / Challenge

The Rural Healthcare Divide

Zero Connectivity

Telemedicine apps assume 4G/5G broadband, failing completely in remote Sub-Centres with zero signal.

Language & UI Literacy

Elderly rural patients cannot operate complex smartphone interfaces. Care must be ASHA-assisted.

Emergency Triage Delay

Chest pain, stroke, and severe respiratory cases lose the golden hour due to absence of local triage rules.

02 / Innovation

Our Offline-First Solution

📱 100% Offline Intake

• Demographic registration hits local storage first
• Vernacular voice intake in 11 languages
• Fully operable in Airplane Mode

🧠 On-Device Triage

• Pure deterministic ICMR clinical decision table
• Zero cloud latency for life-threatening cases
• Stat Aspirin/ACS guidance instantly

⚡ 3-State Sync Queue

• 🔴 Offline → 🟡 Syncing → 🟢 Synced
• Background sync whenever signal blips
• Automatic conflict resolution

05 / Clinical e-Rx

Automated Medical Prescriptions & PDF

Standardized ICMR / NLEM PDF

• Automatically formulates generic NLEM drugs based on symptoms and pediatric vs adult age.
• Includes official digital signature stamp, MCI registration, and ABHA QR code.
• 1-Click download for both Doctor and Patient.

Vernacular Audio Readout

• Solves rural prescription illiteracy.
• Patient clicks 🔊 to hear morning/afternoon/night dosages and dietary instructions spoken in their mother tongue (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.).
